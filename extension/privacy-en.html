Privacy and help

Your groups and settings stay in your browser.

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

Getting started

  1. Sign in to Facebook directly on its website.
  2. Open the Facebook Unhook menu and select “Read my groups” or “Refresh”.
  3. Keep the “Your groups” tab open until reading is complete, then return to your feed.

Before the first successful refresh, the group filter hides recognized posts. You can restore them immediately by turning off “Only my groups” or the extension. Reload any already open Facebook tabs after installation.

Use the globe icon in the menu to choose English, Polish or Automatic. Automatic uses the browser’s interface language: Polish for Polish, and English for other languages.

What the extension reads

On www.facebook.com, the extension locally analyzes page elements: post headings and links, the current view’s address, and interface elements such as reels, stories, navigation and contacts. This allows it to filter the feed and hide selected parts of the page. Post content, comments and the contact list are not saved in extension storage.

When you refresh the group list, the extension opens and scrolls “Your groups”. It reads group identifiers, names, addresses and membership status from the visible list, embedded page data and responses already being retrieved by Facebook. Temporarily analyzed responses may contain other data; only group records needed for filtering are retained. It does not automatically visit each group or send its own requests to Facebook’s API.

What is saved

  • Group names, identifiers, alternative addresses and your choices about post visibility.
  • Feature settings, your language preference, and the date and status of the last group-list refresh.

Data is kept in the extension’s local storage in your Microsoft Edge profile. We do not use cloud synchronization for extension data. The list belongs to your browser profile, not your Facebook account: refresh it after switching accounts or joining or leaving a group.

Data transmission and other services

The extension has no server of its own, analytics, telemetry, advertising, payments or donation links. It does not send the data it reads to the publisher or other recipients, and does not sell data. It does not read passwords, cookies or login tokens. No separate account is required.

Facebook continues making its normal network requests as you use and scroll its website; these are governed by Facebook’s policies. Installing and updating the extension through the store is governed by Microsoft Edge Add-ons policies. This policy describes Facebook Unhook itself.

Control and deletion

You can turn off individual features or the entire extension from its menu. Turning it off does not delete the saved list. Uninstalling the extension at edge://extensions removes its local data from that browser profile. The publisher has no access to this storage and cannot remotely read, change or delete your settings.

Limitations

The desktop version of www.facebook.com with a Polish or English interface is supported. The extension menu is available in English and Polish. The extension filters content already loaded by Facebook; it does not retrieve every post or guarantee their order. Facebook interface changes may require an extension update. An incomplete group-list refresh preserves the previous list.

If posts are missing, check your group list and visibility switches, refresh the list, or turn off the filter. No switch changes your group memberships or Facebook account settings.
